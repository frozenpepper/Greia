# Greia launch video: script (v1 draft)

Mode: **Original** (launch-video-clone skill). Category: **ui-showcase** (the real Shell and Activities doing real work),
with one **editorial** beat (the Acer photo). Look: the Greia night sky (navy, constellation lines, butter/mint/violet stars)
turning into the warm cream/butter Shell world. Type: Nunito Sans (the project's own font), never a default stack.

Format: 1920x1080, 30 fps, 48 s = 1440 f. 100 BPM -> a beat every 18 f, a bar every 72 f (2.4 s). Voice-over in English.

## Source facts (verified in the repo, 2026-10-17)

- 45 built-in Activities (activities/), EN + IT complete, ES/FR staged. Ages: most 3-7, one from 2.
- Every child picks an explicit profile at boot. Platform-owned Home button on every Activity.
- Works offline; no browser, no desktop, no ads. Activities sandboxed.
- Reference floor: first-generation Acer Aspire One (Atom, ~1 GB RAM, 1024x600). Raspberry Pi 400/500 first-class.
- Greia Parents: free web client on the local network (profiles, time limits, which Activities a child sees). Native iOS/Android
  companion exists in development: say "coming soon".
- Activities are made by agents + people from the public authoring skill; voices are recorded at authoring time (no AI on the child PC).
- Open source (MPL-2.0), pre-release 0.13.0, ~4,600 commits since 2026-08-08. Do not say "available now".
