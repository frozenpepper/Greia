## Accepted pilot claims

1. Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and the second-largest planet in the solar system.
2. Saturn is a gas giant made mostly of hydrogen and helium; it does not have a solid surface like Earth.
3. Saturn's rings are not solid hoops. They contain billions of separate small pieces of ice and rock/dust, orbiting Saturn.
4. Saturn's rotation axis is tilted by about 26.7 degrees. Its rings lie around the equator, so the rings share that tilt; as Saturn moves around the Sun, Earth observers see its rings at changing angles.

The first child page does not use Saturn's currently confirmed moon count because that catalogue can change as discoveries/confirmations change. A future moon-specific page may own a separately reviewed count if educationally useful.

## Child-facing wording contract

### Intro — Leo

**Italian**  
`Ecco Saturno, il gigante con gli anelli! Tocca il pianeta e i suoi anelli per scoprire come sono fatti.`

**English**  
`Here is Saturn, the giant with rings! Touch the planet and its rings to discover what they are made of.`

### Place and size — Leo

**Italian**  
`Saturno è il sesto pianeta dal Sole ed è il secondo pianeta più grande del nostro Sistema Solare.`

**English**  
`Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and the second-largest planet in our solar system.`

### Gas giant — Leo

**Italian**  
`Saturno è un gigante gassoso fatto soprattutto di idrogeno ed elio. Non ha una superficie solida come la Terra.`

**English**  
`Saturn is a gas giant made mostly of hydrogen and helium. It does not have a solid surface like Earth.`

### Rings — Leo

**Italian**  
`Gli anelli di Saturno non sono cerchi solidi. Sono formati da miliardi di pezzetti di ghiaccio e roccia che orbitano intorno al pianeta.`

**English**  
`Saturn's rings are not solid hoops. They are made of billions of pieces of ice and rock orbiting the planet.`

### Tilt — Leo

**Italian**  
`Saturno è inclinato e i suoi anelli sono inclinati con lui. Mentre gira intorno al Sole, dalla Terra vediamo gli anelli da angolazioni diverse.`

**English**  
`Saturn is tilted, and its rings are tilted with it. As Saturn travels around the Sun, from Earth we see the rings at different angles.`

## Interaction-to-claim mapping

- solar-system/size hotspot -> mini Sun-to-Saturn position indicator + Earth/Saturn size comparison;
- gas hotspot -> cloud-band movement + gas-giant narration;
- ring hotspot -> reveal separate orbiting ring particles rather than a solid ring;
- tilt hotspot -> cycle clearly different ring viewing angles while Saturn remains central.

The page uses lightweight mathematical animation only. It is not an orbital/atmospheric simulation and must not imply physical scale fidelity.

## Scope guardrails

- Do not depict Saturn's rings as solid hoops or a continuous rigid disk; they are made of many separate orbiting particles.
- Do not depict Saturn as having a solid walkable surface like Earth; it is a gas giant.
- Do not teach a hard-coded current moon count in this v0.1 page because the confirmed catalogue can change.
- Do not imply that the schematic size, distance, orbit speed, ring thickness, or viewing-angle animation is physically to scale.
- Do not imply that the changing apparent ring angle means the rings themselves detach, wobble independently, or change their equatorial orientation around Saturn.
