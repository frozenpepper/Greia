## Accepted claims

### The farthest major planet

Neptune is the eighth and most distant major planet from the Sun, averaging about 30 astronomical units away. Sunlight takes roughly four hours to reach Neptune.

### A very long year

Neptune takes about 165 Earth years to complete one orbit around the Sun. A Neptune day is much shorter, about 16 hours.

### The solar system's strongest planetary winds

NASA describes Neptune as the windiest world in the solar system. Winds can exceed 1,200 miles per hour / 2,000 kilometers per hour, faster than the speed of sound on Earth.

### A blue ice giant with no solid surface

Neptune is an ice giant. Its atmosphere is mainly hydrogen and helium with methane; methane contributes to its blue appearance. Neptune does not have an Earth-like solid surface. It also has faint rings.

## Scope guardrails

- Do not portray Neptune as a solid blue ocean world; its visible blue layers are atmosphere/clouds over a deep ice-giant interior.
- Do not imply methane alone explains every detail of Neptune's color; use wording that it contributes to the blue appearance.
- Avoid current moon counts.
- Do not freeze one storm such as the Great Dark Spot as a permanent feature; large storms can appear and disappear.
- Ring brightness may be exaggerated schematically for visibility, but narration should still call them faint.

## Approved narration wording

### `encyclopedia.space.neptune.intro`

**it:** Ecco Nettuno, l'ottavo e più lontano dei pianeti principali. È un gigante ghiacciato blu, freddo e pieno di venti potentissimi.

**en:** Here is Neptune, the eighth and most distant major planet. It is a cold blue ice giant with incredibly powerful winds.

### `encyclopedia.space.neptune.distance`

**it:** Nettuno è circa trenta volte più lontano dal Sole della Terra. La luce del Sole impiega circa quattro ore per arrivare fin laggiù.

**en:** Neptune is about thirty times farther from the Sun than Earth is. Sunlight takes roughly four hours to travel all the way there.

### `encyclopedia.space.neptune.year`

**it:** Nettuno gira su se stesso in circa sedici ore, ma per fare un intero giro intorno al Sole impiega circa centosessantacinque anni terrestri.

**en:** Neptune spins once in about sixteen hours, but it takes about one hundred sixty-five Earth years to complete one orbit around the Sun.

### `encyclopedia.space.neptune.winds`

**it:** Nettuno è il mondo più ventoso del Sistema Solare. Alcuni venti superano i duemila chilometri all'ora.

**en:** Neptune is the windiest world in the solar system. Some winds race faster than two thousand kilometers per hour.

### `encyclopedia.space.neptune.blue`

**it:** Nettuno è un gigante ghiacciato senza una superficie solida come la Terra. Il metano nella sua atmosfera contribuisce al colore blu, e intorno al pianeta ci sono anche anelli molto deboli.

**en:** Neptune is an ice giant without a solid surface like Earth's. Methane in its atmosphere contributes to the blue color, and faint rings also circle the planet.
