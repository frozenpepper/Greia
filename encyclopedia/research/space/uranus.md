## Accepted claims

### A planet tipped on its side

Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun. Its rotation axis is tilted by about 97.8 degrees, so it appears to spin almost on its side rather than upright like most planets.

### Very long, extreme seasons

Uranus takes about 84 Earth years to orbit the Sun. Because of its unusual tilt, each pole can spend roughly a quarter of a Uranian year pointed toward the Sun while the opposite side experiences a very long dark season.

### An ice giant with no true surface

Uranus is an ice giant, not a rocky world with a walkable surface. Most of the planet is fluid material beneath an atmosphere made mainly of hydrogen and helium with methane and traces of other compounds.

### Blue-green color and rings

Methane absorbs red light and contributes to Uranus's blue-green appearance. Uranus also has a system of faint rings. Avoid volatile moon-count trivia in the child entry.

## Scope guardrails

- Do not say Uranus literally rolls along a solid orbital track; the rolling-ball image is only a visual analogy for its axial orientation.
- Do not claim the cause of the extreme tilt is known with certainty. A giant collision is one hypothesis.
- Do not depict a solid surface a spacecraft could land on.
- Avoid current moon counts.
- Keep ring visibility exaggerated only as a teaching aid and label it as schematic through presentation, not narration.

## Approved narration wording

### `encyclopedia.space.uranus.intro`

**it:** Questo è Urano, un gigante ghiacciato che sembra girare quasi sdraiato su un fianco. È uno dei mondi più strani del Sistema Solare.

**en:** This is Uranus, an ice giant that appears to spin almost on its side. It is one of the strangest-looking worlds in the solar system.

### `encyclopedia.space.uranus.tilt`

**it:** L'asse di Urano è inclinato di quasi novantotto gradi. Per questo, mentre ruota, il pianeta sembra quasi rotolare lungo la sua orbita.

**en:** Uranus's axis is tilted by almost ninety-eight degrees. As it spins, the planet therefore looks almost as if it is rolling along its orbit.

### `encyclopedia.space.uranus.seasons`

**it:** Un anno su Urano dura circa ottantaquattro anni terrestri. Con il pianeta così inclinato, le stagioni sono lunghissime e un polo può restare rivolto verso il Sole per molti anni.

**en:** A year on Uranus lasts about eighty-four Earth years. With the planet tilted so far sideways, its seasons are extremely long and one pole can face the Sun for many years.

### `encyclopedia.space.uranus.color`

**it:** L'atmosfera di Urano contiene metano. Questo gas assorbe parte della luce rossa e contribuisce al colore azzurro-verde del pianeta.

**en:** Uranus's atmosphere contains methane. This gas absorbs some red light and helps give the planet its blue-green color.

### `encyclopedia.space.uranus.rings`

**it:** Anche Urano ha degli anelli. Sono molto più deboli e scuri di quelli luminosi di Saturno, ma circondano comunque il pianeta.

**en:** Uranus has rings too. They are much fainter and darker than Saturn's bright rings, but they still circle the planet.
