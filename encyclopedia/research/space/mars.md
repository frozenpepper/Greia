## Accepted claims

### The red planet

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun. Iron minerals in Martian soil oxidize — rust — and help make the surface look reddish.

### A familiar-length day

Mars rotates once in about 24.6 hours, very close to an Earth day. A Martian solar day is called a sol. Mars is tilted by about 25 degrees, so it also has seasons, although its seasons last longer because its year is longer.

### A wetter ancient world

NASA missions have found abundant evidence that Mars was much wetter and warmer billions of years ago. Ancient river valleys, deltas and lakebeds show that liquid water once shaped the surface. Present-day surface conditions are very different; do not depict modern Mars as having open rivers or lakes.

### A gigantic volcano

Olympus Mons is the largest volcano in the solar system. NASA describes it as more than 25 miles / 40 km tall from base to summit. The child-facing page should communicate extraordinary scale schematically, not pretend its drawing is geographically to scale.

## Scope guardrails

- Do not teach that Mars is red because it is hot; the color is linked to oxidized iron minerals.
- Do not imply liquid rivers are flowing across Mars today.
- Do not claim present or past life has been discovered on Mars.
- Avoid changing mission-status trivia and volatile rover counts.
- Treat size comparisons and elevation graphics as schematic.

## Approved narration wording

### `encyclopedia.space.mars.intro`

**it:** Questo è Marte, il pianeta rosso. Il suo terreno contiene minerali di ferro che si sono ossidati, un po' come la ruggine, e gli danno quel colore speciale.

**en:** This is Mars, the red planet. Its soil contains iron minerals that oxidized, a little like rust, helping give the surface its special color.

### `encyclopedia.space.mars.red`

**it:** Marte non è rosso perché brucia. Nel terreno ci sono minerali ricchi di ferro che si sono ossidati: per questo molta polvere e molte rocce appaiono rossastre.

**en:** Mars is not red because it is burning. Iron-rich minerals in the ground oxidized, so much of the dust and rock looks reddish.

### `encyclopedia.space.mars.day`

**it:** Un giorno su Marte dura circa ventiquattro ore e mezza, quasi come sulla Terra. I giorni marziani si chiamano sol.

**en:** A day on Mars lasts about twenty-four and a half hours, almost like an Earth day. Martian solar days are called sols.

### `encyclopedia.space.mars.water`

**it:** Oggi Marte è freddo e molto secco, ma antiche valli, delta e letti di laghi mostrano che miliardi di anni fa molta acqua liquida scorreva sulla sua superficie.

**en:** Mars is cold and very dry today, but ancient valleys, deltas, and lakebeds show that lots of liquid water flowed across its surface billions of years ago.

### `encyclopedia.space.mars.olympus`

**it:** Su Marte c'è Olympus Mons, il vulcano più grande del Sistema Solare. Dalla base alla cima supera i quaranta chilometri di altezza.

**en:** Mars is home to Olympus Mons, the largest volcano in the solar system. From its base to its summit it rises more than forty kilometers.
