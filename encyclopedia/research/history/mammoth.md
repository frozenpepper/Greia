## Accepted claims

### Woolly mammoths were elephant relatives

Woolly mammoths (`Mammuthus primigenius`) belonged to the elephant family. They were not dinosaurs and were not simply an early stage of a modern elephant species. The page may compare a mammoth and a modern elephant silhouette to show shared body plan plus cold-climate adaptations.

### Built for cold environments

Woolly mammoths lived across cold tundra and steppe regions of Eurasia and northern North America. Thick fur and a substantial layer of body fat helped insulate them. Their ears were smaller than those of many living elephants, reducing exposed surface area in cold conditions.

### Tusks and plant diet

Woolly mammoths had long curved tusks and ridged molars suited to plant foods. Tusks could be used in several ways, including moving material and social/combat behavior; v0.1 should avoid inventing one universal use.

### Mammoths lived much later than non-avian dinosaurs

Non-avian dinosaurs disappeared about 66 million years ago. Woolly mammoths lived during the much more recent Ice Age and overlapped in time with humans. Isolated woolly mammoth populations survived until only about 4,000 years ago.

## Scope guardrails

- Never classify mammoths as dinosaurs.
- Do not teach that mammoths were direct ancestors of modern elephants; call them relatives within the elephant family.
- Do not imply every mammoth species was woolly or lived on permanent ice.
- Do not depict mammoths living alongside non-avian dinosaurs.
- Avoid a single-cause extinction story; climate and human pressures varied over populations and time.
- Keep the body comparison schematic rather than pretending exact scale from one specimen.

## Approved narration wording

### `encyclopedia.history.mammoth.intro`

**it:** Questo è un mammut lanoso, un parente estinto degli elefanti. Viveva in ambienti freddi e aveva un corpo pieno di adattamenti per trattenere il calore.

**en:** This is a woolly mammoth, an extinct relative of elephants. It lived in cold environments and had several body adaptations that helped conserve heat.

### `encyclopedia.history.mammoth.coat`

**it:** Il mammut lanoso aveva una folta pelliccia e uno spesso strato di grasso sotto la pelle. Insieme aiutavano a isolare il corpo dal freddo.

**en:** The woolly mammoth had a thick coat and a substantial layer of fat under its skin. Together they helped insulate its body from the cold.

### `encyclopedia.history.mammoth.ears`

**it:** Le orecchie del mammut lanoso erano relativamente piccole. In un clima freddo, avere meno superficie esposta aiuta a perdere meno calore.

**en:** Woolly mammoth ears were relatively small. In a cold climate, having less exposed surface area helps reduce heat loss.

### `encyclopedia.history.mammoth.elephants`

**it:** Mammut ed elefanti appartengono alla stessa grande famiglia, ma il mammut lanoso non era semplicemente un elefante moderno con più pelo: era una specie distinta e oggi estinta.

**en:** Mammoths and elephants belong to the same broad family, but a woolly mammoth was not simply a modern elephant with more hair. It was a distinct species that is now extinct.

### `encyclopedia.history.mammoth.time`

**it:** I mammut lanosi vissero moltissimo tempo dopo i grandi dinosauri non aviani e incontrarono anche gli esseri umani. Alcune popolazioni isolate sopravvissero fino a circa quattromila anni fa.

**en:** Woolly mammoths lived vastly later than the large non-avian dinosaurs and even overlapped with humans. Some isolated populations survived until about four thousand years ago.
