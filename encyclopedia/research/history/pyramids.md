## Accepted claims

### Pyramids were monumental royal tombs and complexes

Ancient Egyptian pyramids were monumental stone structures associated especially with royal burials. Pyramid building was at its height during Egypt's Old Kingdom, particularly the Fourth through Sixth Dynasties. The visible pyramid was part of a broader funerary complex rather than an isolated mysterious object.

### They were human engineering projects

Pyramids were designed, quarried, transported and assembled by people using the engineering knowledge and tools available at the time. Quarry marks and worker evidence show organized work gangs, skilled craftspeople and additional labor crews. There is no scientific basis for alien-builder stories.

### The simple “built by slaves” story is misleading

Evidence around Giza points to a large organized workforce containing skilled year-round workers and additional labor crews. Ancient labor systems were complex and unequal, but the child page should not repeat the popular one-line claim that the pyramids were simply built by masses of chained slaves.

### Construction methods are partly reconstructed from evidence

Stone came from local and more distant quarries. Sledges, ramps, leveling techniques and careful surveying are supported by archaeological and textual evidence, but not every construction detail is known with certainty. The v0.1 stacking interaction is schematic and must not be narrated as the one proven exact building sequence.

## Scope guardrails

- No aliens, lost supertechnology or supernatural construction claims.
- Do not teach “slaves built the pyramids” as a simple established fact.
- Do not claim every pyramid was built exactly the same way.
- Do not imply the child-facing block-stacking interaction is a literal reconstruction of the engineering sequence.
- Do not conflate Egyptian pyramids with pyramids from unrelated cultures around the world.
- Keep burial imagery non-graphic and child-safe.

## Approved narration wording

### `encyclopedia.history.pyramids.intro`

**it:** Le piramidi dell'antico Egitto sono giganteschi monumenti di pietra costruiti dagli esseri umani migliaia di anni fa. Molte furono create come tombe reali dentro grandi complessi funerari.

**en:** The pyramids of ancient Egypt are enormous stone monuments built by people thousands of years ago. Many were created as royal tombs within larger funerary complexes.

### `encyclopedia.history.pyramids.blocks`

**it:** Una grande piramide richiedeva moltissimi blocchi di pietra preparati, trasportati e messi in posizione con grande precisione. Il nostro gioco di strati è solo uno schema, non il vero cantiere in miniatura.

**en:** A great pyramid required huge numbers of prepared stone blocks to be transported and positioned carefully. Our layer-stacking game is only a diagram, not a miniature copy of the real building process.

### `encyclopedia.history.pyramids.workers`

**it:** Le piramidi furono costruite da squadre organizzate di persone: artigiani, cavatori, trasportatori, muratori e altri lavoratori. Le prove archeologiche non raccontano la semplice storia di una massa di schiavi incatenati.

**en:** Pyramids were built by organized teams of people: craftspeople, quarry workers, haulers, masons, and other laborers. Archaeological evidence does not support the simple story of one mass of chained slaves.

### `encyclopedia.history.pyramids.inside`

**it:** Dentro e intorno alle piramidi c'erano passaggi, camere e altri edifici del complesso funerario. Le grandi piramidi non erano montagne cave piene di stanze segrete ovunque.

**en:** Inside and around pyramids were passages, chambers, and other buildings belonging to the funerary complex. Great pyramids were not hollow mountains filled everywhere with secret rooms.

### `encyclopedia.history.pyramids.engineering`

**it:** Gli antichi Egizi usavano cave, slitte, rampe, misure e molta organizzazione per muovere e posizionare la pietra. Gli archeologi ricostruiscono questi metodi dalle prove, ma alcuni dettagli del lavoro restano ancora discussi.

**en:** Ancient Egyptians used quarries, sledges, ramps, measurement, and extensive organization to move and place stone. Archaeologists reconstruct these methods from evidence, while some construction details are still debated.
