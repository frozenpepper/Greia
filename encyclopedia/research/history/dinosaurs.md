## Accepted claims

### Dinosaurs were a very diverse group

Dinosaurs were not one kind of animal. They included two-legged and four-legged forms, meat-eaters and plant-eaters, tiny animals and enormous ones. The v0.1 page may compare three broad representative silhouettes to communicate diversity without pretending those three species represent every dinosaur.

### Non-avian dinosaurs disappeared about 66 million years ago

At the end of the Cretaceous Period, about 66 million years ago, a global catastrophe associated with a large asteroid impact contributed to the extinction of all non-avian dinosaurs. Humans appeared vastly later. The child page should never place humans and non-avian dinosaurs together.

### Birds are living dinosaurs

Modern birds are the surviving dinosaur lineage. It is accurate and useful to tell children that birds are living dinosaurs, while explaining that the huge familiar non-avian forms such as Tyrannosaurus and Triceratops are extinct.

### Fossils are evidence, not photographs

Scientists reconstruct dinosaur bodies, movement and behavior from fossils and comparisons with living animals. Details such as colors are often uncertain. The page should use clearly schematic Greia illustrations and avoid presenting speculative coloration as known fact.

## Scope guardrails

- Never show humans living alongside non-avian dinosaurs.
- Do not say "all dinosaurs went extinct" without the bird qualification.
- Do not portray every dinosaur as giant, scaly, roaring or carnivorous.
- Do not claim one exact color pattern for a species unless unusually strong evidence exists; v0.1 uses schematic colors.
- Do not teach pterosaurs or marine reptiles as dinosaurs.
- Avoid species-by-species encyclopedic expansion in v0.1; that is explicitly deferred.

## Approved narration wording

### `encyclopedia.history.dinosaurs.intro`

**it:** I dinosauri non erano tutti uguali. Ce n'erano di piccoli e giganteschi, a due o quattro zampe, mangiatori di piante e mangiatori di altri animali. Facciamo un salto indietro nel tempo!

**en:** Dinosaurs were not all alike. Some were small and some enormous, some walked on two legs or four, and they included plant-eaters and meat-eaters. Let's travel far back in time!

### `encyclopedia.history.dinosaurs.diversity`

**it:** Guarda quanto erano diversi. Un tirannosauro camminava su due zampe ed era carnivoro, un Triceratops era erbivoro e camminava su quattro zampe, e i sauropodi avevano colli e code lunghissimi.

**en:** Look how different they could be. A tyrannosaur walked on two legs and ate meat, Triceratops was a four-legged plant-eater, and sauropods had extremely long necks and tails.

### `encyclopedia.history.dinosaurs.time`

**it:** I dinosauri non aviani scomparvero circa sessantasei milioni di anni fa. Gli esseri umani arrivarono moltissimo tempo dopo: persone e tirannosauri non hanno mai vissuto insieme.

**en:** Non-avian dinosaurs disappeared about sixty-six million years ago. Humans arrived vastly later, so people and tyrannosaurs never lived together.

### `encyclopedia.history.dinosaurs.birds`

**it:** Però una parte della storia dei dinosauri continua ancora oggi: gli uccelli sono dinosauri viventi. Quando guardi un passero o una gallina, stai guardando un ramo sopravvissuto dei dinosauri.

**en:** But part of the dinosaur story is still alive today: birds are living dinosaurs. When you look at a sparrow or a chicken, you are seeing a surviving branch of the dinosaur family.

### `encyclopedia.history.dinosaurs.evidence`

**it:** I paleontologi ricostruiscono i dinosauri usando fossili: ossa, denti, impronte e altri indizi. Alcuni dettagli, come molti colori della pelle, restano incerti e possono cambiare quando troviamo nuove prove.

**en:** Paleontologists reconstruct dinosaurs from fossils such as bones, teeth, footprints, and other clues. Some details, including many skin colors, remain uncertain and can change when new evidence is found.
