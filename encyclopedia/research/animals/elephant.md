The page is a generic elephant introduction. Where a source describes Asian elephants specifically, the runtime wording uses only anatomy/behavior that the cited Smithsonian material presents as elephant traits, or explicitly avoids species-specific numerical claims.

## Source set

### Smithsonian's National Zoo — Asian elephant

- `https://nationalzoo.si.edu/animals/asian-elephant`

Supported concepts used here:

- the trunk is a fusion of the nose and upper lip and contains no bones;
- the trunk is highly muscular/flexible and is used for smelling, touching, feeding, drinking, lifting and communication;
- elephants take up water with the trunk and squirt it into the mouth;
- large ears contain many blood vessels and ear flapping helps cool the animal;
- elephants are highly social and trunk touch is an important communication behavior.

### Smithsonian's National Zoo — Elephant Cam / elephant social material

- `https://nationalzoo.si.edu/webcams/elephants`
- `https://nationalzoo.si.edu/animals/news/elephant-calf-update-building-bonds-among-national-zoos-herd`

Supporting concepts:

- elephants form close social bonds;
- they use trunks to pick up food, strip bark and explore;
- ear flapping can help with cooling;
- gentle trunk touches are used during social interactions.

## Accepted claims

1. **Versatile trunk.** An elephant's trunk combines nose and upper lip and is a powerful, flexible organ used for smelling, touching, feeding, drinking and manipulating objects.
2. **Water.** Elephants can draw water into the trunk and then squirt it into the mouth; the trunk is not a drinking straw directly to the stomach.
3. **Cooling ears.** Large ears contain many blood vessels, and flapping them helps move heat away and cool the animal.
4. **Social touch.** Elephants are highly social and frequently use trunk touches as part of communication and greeting.

## Child-facing wording contract

### Intro — Tito

**Italian**  
`Ecco l'elefante! La proboscide è forte e delicata, le grandi orecchie aiutano a rinfrescarsi e il contatto serve anche per comunicare.`

**English**  
`Here is the elephant! Its trunk is strong and delicate, its large ears help it cool down, and touch is also part of elephant communication.`

### Trunk — Tito

**Italian**  
`La proboscide unisce naso e labbro superiore. È piena di muscoli e serve per annusare, toccare, prendere il cibo e spostare oggetti.`

**English**  
`The trunk combines the nose and upper lip. It is packed with muscles and is used to smell, touch, pick up food, and move objects.`

### Water — Tito

**Italian**  
`L'elefante aspira l'acqua nella proboscide e poi la spruzza dentro la bocca per bere. Può usarla anche per fare il bagno.`

**English**  
`An elephant draws water into its trunk and then squirts it into its mouth to drink. It can also use the trunk for bathing.`

### Ears — Tito

**Italian**  
`Le grandi orecchie hanno molti vasi sanguigni. Quando l'elefante le muove, possono aiutarlo a disperdere calore e rinfrescarsi.`

**English**  
`Large elephant ears contain many blood vessels. Flapping them can help move heat away and cool the elephant.`

### Social touch — Tito

**Italian**  
`Gli elefanti sono animali molto sociali. Si toccano spesso con la proboscide per salutarsi, esplorare e comunicare.`

**English**  
`Elephants are very social animals. They often touch with their trunks to greet, explore, and communicate.`

## Interaction-to-claim mapping

- trunk hotspot -> animate a flexible trunk reaching toward two nearby objects;
- water hotspot -> bounded water uptake/spray arc ending at the mouth/body rather than implying the trunk is the stomach route;
- ears hotspot -> increase a slow ear-flap animation and show a subtle cool-air visual;
- social hotspot -> reveal a second small elephant and animate a gentle trunk-to-trunk touch.

## Scope guardrails

- Do not teach species-specific tusk/ear-size differences on this generic first page.
- Do not use exact lifespan, body weight or herd hierarchy claims unless a later species-specific layer owns them.
- Do not anthropomorphize trunk touches as human-style emotions without evidence.
- Keep water spray bounded and cheap; no fluid simulation.
