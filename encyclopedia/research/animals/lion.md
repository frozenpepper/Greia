## Source set

### Smithsonian's National Zoo and Conservation Biology Institute — Lion

- `https://nationalzoo.si.edu/animals/lion`

Supported concepts used here:

- lions are the most social felines and commonly live in groups called prides;
- adult males have shaggy manes; mane length/color are influenced by age, genetics and hormones;
- lions have strong compact bodies and powerful forelegs, teeth and jaws;
- lions use roaring in territorial/social communication and roars carry over long distances.

### Smithsonian's National Zoo — Do Lions Actually Purr? And Other Questions, Answered

- `https://nationalzoo.si.edu/animals/news/do-lions-actually-purr-and-other-questions-answered`

Supporting concepts:

- pride members remain in communication using roars;
- lions can cooperate when hunting;
- roaring is enabled by big-cat throat anatomy rather than the purring mechanism of smaller cats.

The first page avoids prey-killing detail, cub mortality statistics and territorial fights. Those facts are not needed for the intended young-child discovery experience.

## Accepted claims

1. **Mane.** Many adult male lions have a shaggy mane around the head/neck. Mane size and color vary.
2. **Social pride.** Lions are unusually social cats and often live in family/social groups called prides.
3. **Roar.** Lions use powerful roars to communicate and mark/defend territory; a roar can carry a long distance.
4. **Strong front body.** Lions have powerful forelegs plus strong teeth/jaws; the page uses the forelegs/paws as the child-safe visual focus.

## Child-facing wording contract

### Intro — Tito

**Italian**  
`Ecco il leone! Guarda la criniera, le zampe forti e il grande ruggito. Tocca i simboli e scopri come vive.`

**English**  
`Here is the lion! Look at its mane, strong front legs, and mighty roar. Touch the symbols and discover how it lives.`

### Mane — Tito

**Italian**  
`Molti leoni maschi adulti hanno una grande criniera intorno alla testa e al collo. Colore e grandezza possono cambiare da un leone all'altro.`

**English**  
`Many adult male lions have a large mane around the head and neck. Its color and size can vary from one lion to another.`

### Pride — Tito

**Italian**  
`I leoni sono i felini più sociali. Spesso vivono in gruppi chiamati branchi, dove diversi leoni restano in contatto e crescono i piccoli.`

**English**  
`Lions are the most social cats. They often live in groups called prides, where several lions stay connected and raise their young.`

### Roar — Tito

**Italian**  
`Il ruggito aiuta i leoni a comunicare e a segnalare il loro territorio. È così potente che può sentirsi da molto lontano.`

**English**  
`A roar helps lions communicate and signal their territory. It is so powerful that it can be heard from very far away.`

### Strong forelegs — Tito

**Italian**  
`Il leone ha zampe anteriori molto forti. Insieme al corpo compatto, lo aiutano a muoversi e ad afferrare con forza.`

**English**  
`A lion has very strong front legs. Together with its compact body, they help it move and grip powerfully.`

## Interaction-to-claim mapping

- mane hotspot -> cycle two clearly stylized mane sizes/colors without implying exact age;
- pride hotspot -> reveal a small bounded group of lion silhouettes beside the main animal;
- roar hotspot -> animate expanding sound-wave rings from the mouth; reserve a semantic real-lion-sound slot for later licensed asset sourcing;
- foreleg/paw hotspot -> enlarge/highlight the front paws and foreleg stance.

## Scope guardrails

- Do not imply all lions have manes; the child line says many adult males.
- Do not claim mane size alone proves health/dominance.
- Do not make violent hunting/killing the page's spectacle.
- Do not ship a random web roar recording. Any real lion sound needs explicit provenance/license review.
