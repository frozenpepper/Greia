Supported concepts used by this pilot:

- the heart is a muscular organ about the size of a fist and pumps blood through the body;
- it has four hollow chambers: two atria and two ventricles;
- veins bring blood toward the heart and arteries carry blood away;
- the right side pumps oxygen-poor blood toward the lungs and the left side pumps oxygen-rich blood toward the body;
- heart valves help blood move in the correct direction and closing valves contribute to the familiar `lub-DUB` sound;
- exercise makes the heart beat faster so more oxygen can reach working muscles.

## Accepted pilot claims

1. **A muscular pump.** The heart is roughly fist-sized and pumps blood through the circulatory system.
2. **Four chambers.** Two upper atria receive blood; two lower ventricles pump blood onward.
3. **Two linked journeys.** Blood travels from the heart to the lungs for oxygen and then from the heart to the rest of the body.
4. **Heartbeat and valves.** Heart muscle contraction pumps blood; closing valves are responsible for the familiar `lub-DUB` sounds.
5. **Faster during exercise.** The heart normally beats faster during exercise to deliver more oxygen to muscles.

The diagram must not use the misleading idea that blood is literally blue inside the body. A purple/darker-red visual may distinguish oxygen-poor blood while captions/narration remain accurate.

## Child-facing wording contract

### Intro — Lilli

**Italian**  
`Il cuore è un muscolo grande più o meno come il tuo pugno. Lavora senza sosta per spingere il sangue in tutto il corpo.`

**English**  
`Your heart is a muscle about the size of your fist. It keeps working to pump blood around your whole body.`

### Four chambers — Lilli

**Italian**  
`Dentro il cuore ci sono quattro camere. Le due in alto ricevono il sangue e le due in basso lo spingono fuori dal cuore.`

**English**  
`Inside the heart there are four chambers. The two on top receive blood, and the two below pump it out of the heart.`

### Lungs and body — Lilli

**Italian**  
`Una parte del cuore manda il sangue ai polmoni per prendere ossigeno. Poi l'altra parte lo spinge verso tutto il corpo.`

**English**  
`One side of the heart sends blood to the lungs to pick up oxygen. Then the other side pumps it out to the rest of the body.`

### Heartbeat and valves — Lilli

**Italian**  
`Ogni battito è una contrazione che spinge il sangue. Le valvole si chiudono e aiutano a fare il famoso suono: lub-dub!`

**English**  
`Each heartbeat is a squeeze that pumps blood. Valves close and help make the familiar sound: lub-dub!`

### Exercise — Lilli

**Italian**  
`Quando corri o giochi con energia, il cuore batte più in fretta per portare più ossigeno ai muscoli.`

**English**  
`When you run or play energetically, your heart beats faster to bring more oxygen to your muscles.`

## Interaction-to-claim mapping

- pump hotspot -> visibly contract/relax the heart and advance small blood markers;
- chamber hotspot -> reveal four clearly separated chambers;
- circulation hotspot -> animate a simplified heart -> lungs -> heart -> body loop;
- heartbeat hotspot -> pulse heart/valves rhythmically and show a visual `lub-dub` double beat;
- exercise hotspot -> temporarily accelerate the otherwise calm animation.

The page should remain schematic and friendly. It is not a diagnostic or anatomically complete medical simulation.

## Scope guardrails

- Do not color oxygen-poor blood as if the blood itself were blue.
- Do not present adult resting heart-rate ranges as a child target; normal rates differ by age and context.
- Do not add disease, blood pressure, medical warnings or fear-based health messaging to the first page.
- Do not claim a perfect one-beat-one-droplet timing relationship from the schematic animation.
