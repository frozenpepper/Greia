## Accepted claims

### Different tooth shapes do different jobs

Incisors at the front have cutting edges. Canines are more pointed and help tear/grip food. Molars have broader chewing surfaces that crush and grind. Primary dentition has incisors, canines and molars; permanent dentition also includes premolars.

### Children have primary teeth before permanent teeth

A typical complete primary dentition contains 20 teeth. Primary teeth are gradually shed and replaced during childhood; permanent teeth appear over a long period. Do not make tooth loss sound alarming or imply every child follows exactly the same timetable.

### Teeth have a hard outer layer and living inner tissue

The visible crown is covered by enamel, a very hard protective tissue. Beneath it are dentin and the inner pulp, which contains nerves and blood vessels. Keep any cutaway schematic friendly and non-graphic.

### Daily care protects teeth

Plaque can contribute to tooth decay. Gentle brushing with fluoride toothpaste helps clean teeth, and fluoride strengthens enamel against decay. Exact toothpaste amounts and age-specific caregiver instructions belong to parents/dentists, not this child-facing discovery page.

## Scope guardrails

- Do not imply every child has the same number of erupted teeth at the same age.
- Do not imply primary teeth are disposable or unimportant; they matter for eating, speech, spacing and oral health.
- Do not teach that canines are only for meat or make diet assumptions.
- Do not present cavities, drilling or tooth loss with fear imagery.
- Do not turn tooth brushing into a scored compliance challenge.
- Do not prescribe age-specific fluoride dosage; keep child-facing care general and defer detailed advice to caregivers/dental professionals.

### `encyclopedia.body.teeth.intro`

**it:** I denti non hanno tutti la stessa forma. Quelli davanti tagliano, i canini aiutano ad afferrare e strappare, e i molari larghi schiacciano e macinano il cibo.

**en:** Your teeth do not all have the same shape. Front teeth cut, canines help grip and tear, and broad molars crush and grind food.

### `encyclopedia.body.teeth.jobs`

**it:** Gli incisivi hanno un bordo adatto a tagliare. I canini sono più appuntiti. I molari hanno superfici larghe che aiutano a schiacciare e masticare.

**en:** Incisors have an edge suited for cutting. Canines are more pointed. Molars have broad surfaces that help crush and chew food.

### `encyclopedia.body.teeth.sets`

**it:** I bambini hanno prima i denti da latte. Un insieme completo ne ha di solito venti. Crescendo, questi denti vengono sostituiti poco alla volta dai denti permanenti.

**en:** Children first have primary, or baby, teeth. A complete set usually has twenty. As you grow, those teeth are gradually replaced by permanent teeth.

### `encyclopedia.body.teeth.layers`

**it:** La parte visibile del dente è coperta dallo smalto, uno strato molto duro che lo protegge. Più all'interno ci sono dentina e polpa, dove arrivano nervi e vasi sanguigni.

**en:** The visible part of a tooth is covered by enamel, a very hard layer that protects it. Deeper inside are dentin and pulp, where nerves and blood vessels are found.

### `encyclopedia.body.teeth.care`

**it:** Spazzolare delicatamente tutti i lati dei denti aiuta a togliere la placca. Il dentifricio al fluoro aiuta a rendere lo smalto più resistente alla carie.

**en:** Gently brushing all sides of your teeth helps remove plaque. Fluoride toothpaste helps make enamel more resistant to tooth decay.
