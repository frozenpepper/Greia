## Accepted claims

### Lungs exchange gases

The lungs are two spongy organs in the chest. When air reaches the tiny alveoli, oxygen moves from the air into nearby blood and carbon dioxide moves from the blood into the alveoli so it can be breathed out.

### Breathing changes chest volume

The diaphragm and chest/rib muscles help move air. During inhalation the diaphragm contracts and moves downward, increasing room in the chest so the lungs expand. During ordinary exhalation those muscles relax and the lungs become smaller as air flows out.

### Airways carry air

Air travels through the nose or mouth, throat and windpipe into branching airways in the lungs before reaching alveoli. The page may simplify the branching tree visually without teaching every anatomical name.

### Activity changes breathing demand

During physical activity the body needs more oxygen and produces more carbon dioxide, so breathing usually becomes faster and deeper. Keep this as a normal body response, not a performance score.

## Scope guardrails

- Do not describe lungs as balloons that inflate themselves; muscles and pressure changes make breathing possible.
- Do not imply inhaled air is pure oxygen; oxygen is one component of air.
- Do not depict oxygen-poor blood as literally blue.
- Do not say we breathe only to "get oxygen"; removing carbon dioxide is also essential.
- Keep alveoli schematic and friendly; no graphic medical imagery.
- Do not make breath-holding, maximal inhalation, or breathing-rate competition into a child challenge.

## Approved narration wording

### `encyclopedia.body.lungs.intro`

**it:** I polmoni sono due organi morbidi nel torace. Quando respiri, l'aria entra ed esce e nei polmoni il sangue prende ossigeno e lascia anidride carbonica.

**en:** Your lungs are two soft organs in your chest. When you breathe, air moves in and out, and inside the lungs your blood picks up oxygen and leaves carbon dioxide behind.

### `encyclopedia.body.lungs.breath`

**it:** Per inspirare, il diaframma sotto i polmoni si contrae e scende, creando più spazio nel torace. Quando si rilassa, lo spazio diminuisce e l'aria può uscire.

**en:** To breathe in, the diaphragm below your lungs contracts and moves downward, making more room in your chest. When it relaxes, the space becomes smaller and air can flow out.

### `encyclopedia.body.lungs.airways`

**it:** L'aria passa dal naso o dalla bocca nelle vie respiratorie. Dentro i polmoni i tubi si dividono in rami sempre più piccoli fino a raggiungere minuscole sacche d'aria.

**en:** Air travels from your nose or mouth into the airways. Inside your lungs, the tubes branch into smaller and smaller passages until they reach tiny air sacs.

### `encyclopedia.body.lungs.exchange`

**it:** Nelle minuscole sacche chiamate alveoli, l'ossigeno passa dall'aria al sangue. Nello stesso momento l'anidride carbonica passa dal sangue all'aria e poi viene espirata.

**en:** In tiny air sacs called alveoli, oxygen moves from the air into your blood. At the same time, carbon dioxide moves from the blood into the air and is then breathed out.

### `encyclopedia.body.lungs.exercise`

**it:** Quando corri o giochi con energia, i muscoli usano più ossigeno e producono più anidride carbonica. Per questo di solito respiri più velocemente e più profondamente.

**en:** When you run or play energetically, your muscles use more oxygen and make more carbon dioxide. That is why you usually breathe faster and more deeply.
