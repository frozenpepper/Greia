## Accepted claims

### Skeleton supports, protects and helps movement

Bones give the body support and shape. Muscles pull on bones to help move the body. Some bones protect important organs: the skull protects the brain and the ribs help protect the heart and lungs.

### Joints connect bones and allow different kinds of movement

A joint is where two or more bones connect. Some joints move a lot, some a little and some are effectively immovable. A child-facing page may contrast a hinge-like elbow with a ball-and-socket shoulder/hip without teaching the full classification system.

### Bone is living tissue

Bone is living tissue that continually changes through remodeling. During childhood, bones grow and become larger and denser. Do not present a skeleton as dry dead material like a museum specimen.

### Bone has layers and marrow

Bone has a dense outer layer and a lighter, lattice-like inner structure. Bone marrow fills spaces inside some bones and produces new blood cells. The page may reveal this with a friendly cutaway.

## Scope guardrails

- Do not teach the adult count of 206 bones as if every child already has exactly that number; bones fuse during growth.
- Do not imply bones move by themselves; muscles and joints work together with bones.
- Do not depict marrow, fractures or internal tissue graphically.
- Do not imply every joint moves the same way.
- Keep the page about normal anatomy and curiosity, not injury fear.

## Approved narration wording

### `encyclopedia.body.bones.intro`

**it:** Sotto la pelle c'è lo scheletro. Le ossa sostengono il corpo, lavorano con i muscoli per farci muovere e alcune proteggono organi importanti.

**en:** Under your skin is your skeleton. Bones support your body, work with muscles to help you move, and some protect important organs.

### `encyclopedia.body.bones.joints`

**it:** Le articolazioni sono i punti dove due o più ossa si incontrano. Alcune, come il gomito, si piegano soprattutto in una direzione; altre, come la spalla, possono muoversi in molte direzioni.

**en:** Joints are places where two or more bones meet. Some, like your elbow, mostly bend one way, while others, like your shoulder, can move in many directions.

### `encyclopedia.body.bones.protect`

**it:** Alcune ossa fanno anche da scudo. Il cranio protegge il cervello e le costole circondano e aiutano a proteggere cuore e polmoni.

**en:** Some bones also act like shields. Your skull protects your brain, and your ribs surround and help protect your heart and lungs.

### `encyclopedia.body.bones.living`

**it:** Le ossa non sono pezzi morti e immobili: sono tessuto vivo. Crescono durante l'infanzia e il corpo continua a rinnovare piccole parti dell'osso per tutta la vita.

**en:** Bones are not dead, unchanging pieces. They are living tissue. They grow during childhood, and your body keeps renewing small parts of bone throughout life.

### `encyclopedia.body.bones.marrow`

**it:** Dentro alcune ossa c'è il midollo osseo. In questo tessuto morbido il corpo produce nuove cellule del sangue.

**en:** Inside some bones is bone marrow. In this soft tissue, your body makes new blood cells.
