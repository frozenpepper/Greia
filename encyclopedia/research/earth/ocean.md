## Accepted claims

### One enormous connected ocean

The ocean covers more than 70 percent of Earth's surface and holds about 97 percent of Earth's water. Named oceans are regions of one connected global ocean.

### Seawater contains dissolved salts

Average seawater salinity is about 35 parts per thousand, although salinity varies by place and conditions. Sodium and chloride are the most abundant dissolved ions, but seawater contains many dissolved substances.

### Light fades rapidly with depth

The upper roughly 200 meters are often called the sunlight/euphotic zone because there is enough light there to support photosynthesis. Between roughly 200 and 1,000 meters is a much dimmer twilight zone. Below about 1,000 meters, sunlight from the surface does not penetrate.

### The deep ocean is still habitat

The deep ocean is dark, cold and under high pressure, yet organisms live there. Do not frame the deep sea as empty or dead merely because sunlight disappears.

## Scope guardrails

- Do not teach that there are five physically separate oceans; named ocean basins are connected parts of one global ocean.
- Do not claim all seawater has exactly the same salinity.
- Do not imply light stops at one sharp universal depth; the page uses schematic depth bands.
- Do not portray deep-sea animals as monsters or make darkness frightening.
- Do not claim that all deep-sea animals make their own light; bioluminescence is common in some deep habitats but not universal.

## Approved narration wording

### `encyclopedia.earth.ocean.intro`

**it:** L'oceano copre più del settanta per cento della Terra. I nomi Pacifico, Atlantico, Indiano, Artico e Meridionale indicano grandi regioni, ma tutta quest'acqua forma un unico oceano collegato.

**en:** The ocean covers more than seventy percent of Earth. Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Arctic, and Southern are names for large regions, but all that water forms one connected global ocean.

### `encyclopedia.earth.ocean.salt`

**it:** L'acqua del mare contiene molti sali disciolti. In media, in mille parti di acqua di mare ce ne sono circa trentacinque di sali, anche se la salinità cambia da luogo a luogo.

**en:** Seawater contains many dissolved salts. On average, about thirty-five parts out of every thousand are dissolved salts, although salinity changes from place to place.

### `encyclopedia.earth.ocean.light`

**it:** La luce del Sole penetra soprattutto nella parte più alta dell'oceano. Sotto circa duecento metri diventa molto più debole e, oltre circa mille metri, la luce del Sole non arriva più.

**en:** Sunlight reaches mainly through the upper ocean. Below about two hundred meters it becomes much weaker, and below roughly one thousand meters sunlight from the surface no longer reaches.

### `encyclopedia.earth.ocean.deep`

**it:** Il mare profondo è buio, freddo e sottoposto a una pressione enorme, ma non è vuoto. Tanti organismi sono adattati a vivere proprio in questi ambienti profondi.

**en:** The deep ocean is dark, cold, and under enormous pressure, but it is not empty. Many organisms are adapted to live in these deep environments.

### `encyclopedia.earth.ocean.water`

**it:** Quasi tutta l'acqua della Terra si trova nell'oceano: circa il novantasette per cento. Il resto è distribuito soprattutto tra ghiaccio, acqua sotterranea, laghi, fiumi e atmosfera.

**en:** Almost all of Earth's water is in the ocean: about ninety-seven percent. The rest is found mostly in ice, groundwater, lakes, rivers, and the atmosphere.
