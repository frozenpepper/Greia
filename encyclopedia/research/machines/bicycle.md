## Accepted claims

### Pedaling transfers motion to the rear wheel

The rider turns the crank with the pedals. A front chainring pulls the chain, the chain turns a rear sprocket, and that turns the rear wheel. The page should show the whole motion path continuously so the child sees cause and effect rather than isolated moving parts.

### Gearing changes the relationship between pedal turns and wheel turns

Different front/rear gear sizes change how far the rear wheel turns for one pedal revolution and how much pedal force is needed. A “harder/faster” gear can move the bicycle farther per pedal turn but usually requires more force; an “easier” gear moves it less far per pedal turn and can make climbing easier.

### Gears do not create free energy

Changing gear ratio trades force and motion. It does not multiply both speed and force for free. Keep this qualitative in the child narration.

### Balance/steering are important but not the v0.1 focus

The entry is specifically about the drivetrain. The drawing may show wheels/frame/handlebars, but it should not pretend the chain explains every part of bicycle motion or stability.

## Scope guardrails

- Do not say the chain directly drives the front wheel on a normal chain-drive bicycle.
- Do not teach that a higher gear is simply “better” or always faster; usefulness depends on effort, terrain and speed.
- Do not imply gears create energy.
- Keep fingers/clothing hazards out of the virtual child interaction; this is a schematic drivetrain.
- Do not turn cycling speed into a child performance score.

## Approved narration wording

### `encyclopedia.machines.bicycle.intro`

**it:** Via! Quando giri i pedali, fai girare la pedivella e la corona. La catena porta quel movimento fino all'ingranaggio della ruota posteriore.

**en:** Go! When you turn the pedals, you turn the crank and front chainring. The chain carries that motion to the gear on the rear wheel.

### `encyclopedia.machines.bicycle.drive`

**it:** Segui il movimento: pedali, corona, catena, pignone posteriore e infine ruota. Ogni parte passa il movimento alla successiva.

**en:** Follow the motion: pedals, chainring, chain, rear sprocket, and finally the wheel. Each part passes motion to the next.

### `encyclopedia.machines.bicycle.ratio`

**it:** Cambiando la grandezza degli ingranaggi cambia il rapporto. Una marcia più facile richiede meno forza ma fa girare meno la ruota per ogni giro dei pedali; una più dura fa il contrario.

**en:** Changing gear sizes changes the ratio. An easier gear needs less force but turns the wheel less for each pedal turn; a harder gear does the opposite.

### `encyclopedia.machines.bicycle.travel`

**it:** Con lo stesso giro completo dei pedali, rapporti diversi possono far girare la ruota posteriore di quantità diverse. Gli ingranaggi cambiano il rapporto tra movimento e forza.

**en:** With the same complete pedal turn, different gear ratios can make the rear wheel turn by different amounts. Gears change the relationship between motion and force.

### `encyclopedia.machines.bicycle.energy`

**it:** Gli ingranaggi non creano energia gratis. Possono rendere più comodo usare più forza o più movimento, ma c'è sempre uno scambio tra le due cose.

**en:** Gears do not create free energy. They can make it more useful to trade force for motion or motion for force, but there is always a tradeoff.
