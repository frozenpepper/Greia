A different first computer

Less “computer stuff.”
More childhood.

Greia is an Activity-first world. Every child has a profile, every Activity has a purpose, and the way Home always belongs to Greia.

01 / Arrive

A world of choices.

The child Shell opens into Create, Play, Learn and Explore. It is a small, familiar map instead of a desktop to manage.

Actual Greia Simulator capture
02 / Make

Ideas become things.

Writing and painting are part of the first Greia experience. A child can begin quickly and keep their creations in their own space.

Actual Greia Simulator capture
03 / Play

Curiosity has room.

Focused Activities can be little experiments, games, puzzles and worlds to explore. A friendly cast helps children recognize the journey.

Actual Greia Simulator capture
04 / Return

Home feels like Home.

Greia owns the Home control above each Activity. The route back remains familiar even when the adventure changes.

A clearer relationship with technology

Playful
at heart.
Thoughtful
underneath.

Greia is designed as an offline-first appliance for children. It protects each child's work, gives parents their own management space, and keeps the underlying Linux system out of the child’s way.
