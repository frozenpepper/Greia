# Batch 1 Target Selection — First-Party Activity Visual Productization

**Date:** 2026-08-22  
**Selection Strategy:** Objective selection of the three weakest remaining first-party Activities based on the whole-catalogue visual audit, excluding already owner-accepted productization references (`shape_garden`, `costruiamo_ponti`, `painting`).

---

## Selected Activities for Batch 1

### 1. `org.greia.treno-numeri` (Il Treno dei Numeri) — Score: 1.9 / 5.0
- **Collection:** `Impara` (Math / Counting & Addition)
- **Why Selected:** Objectively the lowest-scoring and most visually primitive activity in the entire catalogue. The locomotive is rendered with plain geometric rectangle blocks, wheels have crude rotating line spokes, and the landscape is composed of bare ellipses. Productizing this activity will transform early-childhood math into a rich, grounded scenic train journey with Dani the robot as the conductor, illustrated steam engine/cabs, tactile fruit crates, and particle steam.
- **Productization Strategy:** `CREATE_ORIGINAL` (rich steam engine, passenger cab, tactile fruit crates) + `REUSE` (hills, clouds, sunbeams, Dani conductor).

### 2. `org.greia.labirinto-magico` (Labirinto Magico) — Score: 1.9 / 5.0
- **Collection:** `Gioca` (Logic & Spatial Maze Adventure)
- **Why Selected:** Tied for lowest score in the catalogue. Currently looks like an ungrounded algorithmic debug screen with raw 1-pixel grid lines and a floating circle for the player. Productizing this activity will elevate spatial puzzle solving into a true magical labyrinth adventure with tactile themed maze walls, Tito the Fox adventurer with natural bobbing physics, glowing crystal star collectibles, and an enchanted swirling portal goal.
- **Productization Strategy:** `CREATE_ORIGINAL` (tactile maze wall tiles, luminous crystal goal portal) + `REUSE` (Tito/Dani explorer character, atmospheric backdrop lighting).

### 3. `org.greia.acquario-magico` (Acquario Magico) — Score: 2.4 / 5.0
- **Collection:** `Scopri / Crea` (Living Ecosystem & Creative Sandbox)
- **Why Selected:** Third lowest score among all remaining un-productized activities. Currently renders as a flat 2D blue box with primitive polygon fish and straight line kelp. Productizing this sandbox will create an enchanting underwater living world with sunlit turquoise surface caustics, layered sandy seabed with coral reefs and seashells, beautifully illustrated animated tropical fish, sea turtles, and glowing bioluminescent jellyfish.
- **Productization Strategy:** `CREATE_ORIGINAL` (rich animated aquatic species: clownfish, sea turtle, octopus, dolphin, pufferfish; coral reefs and seabed) + `REUSE` (sunbeams, seabed rocks).
