## Decision

Batch 1 is accepted.

I reviewed the whole-catalogue contact sheet/audit and the curated before/after evidence for:

- `org.greia.treno-numeri`
- `org.greia.labirinto-magico`
- `org.greia.acquario-magico`

All three show a material product-quality uplift rather than a cosmetic evidence-only change.

## What is accepted

### Il Treno dei Numeri

The transition from flat programmer-art to a coherent illustrated train scene is substantial. The locomotive/wagon family, cargo objects, Dani integration and countryside treatment improve hierarchy, grounding and delight while preserving the underlying number-game role.

### Labirinto Magico

The original maze was visually close to a technical prototype. The new Tito explorer treatment, beveled maze vocabulary, crystal collectibles, portal goal, atmospheric particles and tactile controls make it read as a real child-facing game without obscuring navigation.

### Acquario Magico

The new illustrated marine fauna, reef/seabed treatment, water lighting and day/night presentation materially improve richness and recognizability while preserving the aquarium interaction model.

## Audit methodology note

The 1–5 scores are useful **internal prioritization heuristics**, not an owner-certified objective quality metric. I accept the ordering and the three Batch 1 selections; I do not treat values such as `4.8` or `4.7` as externally meaningful KPIs.

The catalogue audit should continue to be used comparatively: actual pixels and product judgment remain authoritative.

One item to keep visible for the next batch: `org.greia.writing` is tied near the bottom of the audit and should not be permanently exempted merely because its current classification says `KEEP_EXISTING_INTENTIONAL`. If it is skipped again, the product reason for preserving its sparse visual treatment must be demonstrated explicitly.
