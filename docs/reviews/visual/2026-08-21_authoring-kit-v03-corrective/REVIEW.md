## 1. Executive Summary

This corrective benchmark strictly addresses the owner review findings from the v0.2 milestone. It focuses on four targeted assets to eliminate circular/balloon-like foliage masses, establish unmistakable at-a-glance differentiation between the Oak and Hollow Tree, ensure 64px runtime survivability for the Upright Bramble, and prove the new non-balloon foliage grammar with a supporting berry bush.

---

## 2. Mandatory Benchmark Questions & Honest Answers

### 1. Is the oak still too balloon-like anywhere?
- **No.** The v0.3 Mature Oak canopy completely discards 4-point circle/ellipse loops in favor of multi-scalloped Bézier billowing contours with varying curvature radii and Gouache form shading that follows organic leaf masses. The boughs visibly support the canopy layers from below.

### 2. Does the hollow still feel like an installed opening anywhere?
- **No.** The trunk wood physically swells into a massive pollarded knuckle bowl that organically encloses the aperture. The hollow features an asymmetrical keyhole profile with a thick wooden rim chamfer, a warm sunlit upper lip, deep vertical cavity falloff into blackness, and an interior floor shelf for character seating.

### 3. Do oak and hollow now differ strongly enough at first glance?
- **Yes.** 
  - The **Mature Oak** has an upright, tall, grand silhouette ($H=512$, high canopy crown) with heavy structural boughs reaching upward and outward.
  - The **Interactive Hollow Tree** is a **squat, ancient pollarded tree** with a short, wide trunk ($H_{trunk} \approx 240$) and a **weeping canopy that cascades downwards on both flanks** framing the hollow.
  - Their overall proportions, canopy flows, and silhouettes are instantly distinguishable at first glance.

### 4. Does the bramble remain readable at 64 px?
- **Yes.** By moving from thin, fragile individual leaflets to solid grouped botanical sprays attached along reinforced arching canes, the v0.3 Upright Bramble retains its fountain-like cane silhouette and yellow buttercup accents at 64px without collapsing into stray dots or lines.

### 5. Which asset is still weakest?
- **The Upright Bramble at 64px:** While it is significantly stronger than v0.2 and clearly retains its grouped silhouette, fine details like the buttercup center dots naturally merge into the yellow flower heads at 64px scale.

### 6. Is v0.3 good enough for provisional use as an Activity-authoring reference, or still experimental?
- **v0.3 is good enough for provisional reference in Activity authoring.** The structural rules for load-bearing boughs, squat pollarded weeping canopies, grouped cane foliage, and scalloped Gouache form lighting provide a solid, repeatable baseline for environment assets in *Nascondino nel Bosco*.
