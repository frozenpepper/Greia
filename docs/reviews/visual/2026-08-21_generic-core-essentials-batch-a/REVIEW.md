## 1. Conservative Self-Assessment

### 1. Cross-Activity Leverage & Semantic Neutrality
- **Tableware (`plate_ceramic_empty`, `bowl_ceramic_empty`, `cup_saucer_ceramic`):** Authored as 100% empty, neutral glazed ceramic vessels. They carry zero baked food, fruit, or liquid, making them immediately usable across dining, tea party, kitchen, picnic, or sorting Activities.
- **Mechanical Manipulatives (`gear_wooden`, `gears_interlocking_pair`, `dial_hands_blank`):** Authored with zero baked numerals, labels, or themed text. The dial features clean 12-position radial hashes and two movable-style teardrop pointer hands, suitable for clocks, timers, pressure gauges, puzzle locks, or speedometers.
- **Reading & Books (`book_open_picture_book`, `books_stack_mixed`):** The open picture book features soft abstract pastel illustration motifs with zero readable language; the book stack features rich cloth and leather bindings with gold spine ribs and ribbon bookmark.

### 2. Architecture & Room Openings
- `door_timber_closed` and `door_timber_ajar` establish Greia's first interior doors. The ajar door is a true 3D angled perspective rendering showing door edge thickness, interior room reveal, and cast door shadow (not a skewed 2D transform).
- `opening_arch_simple` provides a neutral molded timber archway for dividing rooms and framing scene transitions.

### 3. Reauthored Legacy Roles (Quality Gaps Closed)
- `furniture.seating.armchair.soft_reading` replaces the flat v0.1 reading chair with a plush, tufted wingback armchair.
- `furniture.storage.bookcase.tall_open` replaces the flat classroom bookcase with a craftsman library unit featuring beadboard backing and molded crown trim.
- `prop.decor.plant.potted_fern` replaces the geometric v0.1 plant with a lush parlor fern in terracotta pot with saucer.
- `prop.storage.basket.woven_empty` replaces the flat basket with a braided wicker storage basket with interior depth and zero baked items.

### 4. Multi-Scale Durability (512px to 64px)
- Rendered proof on `06-runtime-sizes.png` demonstrates that spindle chair, open picture book, wicker basket, ceramic plate, wooden gear, and blank dial retain clean, recognizable silhouettes down to 64px.
