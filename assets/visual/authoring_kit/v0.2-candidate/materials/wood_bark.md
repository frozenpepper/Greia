## 1. Structural Load-Bearing Logic

Wood in Greia represents warmth, organic growth, stability, and child-safe architecture.

### Core Rules:
1. **Weight & Load-Bearing Proportion:**
   - Trunk thickness and bough girth must visually support the apparent volume and weight of the foliage above it.
   - Trunks must visibly branch out into structural boughs that physically enter and support visible foliage masses (branches must never end abruptly in open air beneath floating leaves).
2. **Organic Root Flare & Soil Integration:**
   - Roots flare naturally into the soil, creating a solid, weighted footing without requiring an artificial straight-cut horizontal line.
   - Flaring roots overlap cast shadows and soil tufts to create firm physical grounding.
3. **Planar Bark Shading & Growth Character:**
   - Use 3–4 harmonious tonal zones across the trunk (lit warm cedar plane, midtone body, shaded edge, ambient root occlusion).
   - Enrich master assets with organic woodgrain lines, knots, growth bulges, and bark ridges that follow the curvature of the living trunk.
