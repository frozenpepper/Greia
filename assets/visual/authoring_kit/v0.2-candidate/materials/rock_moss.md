## 1. Geological Massing & Erosion Logic

Stones and boulders in Greia are ancient, friendly, weathered river and glacial formations.

### Core Rules:
1. **Geological Asymmetry over Stacked Steps:**
   - Never construct stone formations as symmetric geometric steps or stacked cakes.
   - Use natural fractured planes, weathered horizontal ledges, and irregular boulder groupings with varying mass and erosion angles.
2. **Soft Planar Value Shifts:**
   - Define stone surfaces through 2–3 clear light-facing and shaded planes in warm slate/graphite tones.
   - Avoid harsh, mechanical black diagonal seams; use soft graphite creases with rounded corners.
3. **Integrated Creeping Velvet Moss:**
   - Moss must not sit as a detached green cap.
   - Moss grows naturally along moisture-catching upper surfaces, stone ledges, and creeps into cracks and crevices following gravity and moisture logic.
