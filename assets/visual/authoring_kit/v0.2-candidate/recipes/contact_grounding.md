## 1. Relational Grounding Principles

Grounding is an outcome of weight, posture, and environmental interaction — never an artificial straight-cut line.

### Key Factors:
1. **Weight-Bearing Root / Base Flare:** Base expands laterally to support the object mass.
2. **Dual-Pass Contact Occlusion:**
   - Tight, dark contact shadow directly under weight-bearing points.
   - Soft, wider ambient shadow spread around the base.
3. **Environmental Overlap:** Foreground grass blades, clovers, or soil overlap slightly across root edges to seat the object naturally into the landscape.
