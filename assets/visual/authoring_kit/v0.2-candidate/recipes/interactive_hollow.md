## 1. Natural Cavity Integration vs. Pasted Door Openings

An interactive hollow in Greia must read as a **natural, living woodland cavity formed by tree growth**, highly readable for child gameplay (e.g. *Nascondino nel Bosco*).

### Construction Principles:
1. **Trunk Deformation & Growth Bulge:**
   - The trunk wood must organically swell and deform around the aperture.
   - Woodgrain lines curve around the cavity rather than passing behind it unbroken.
2. **Asymmetrical Outer Bark Rim:**
   - The opening must have an organic, asymmetrical silhouette (never a regular oval).
   - Thick, beveled wooden lip with varying rim thickness around the perimeter.
3. **Light & Depth Hierarchy:**
   - **Sunlit Upper Lip:** Warm honey highlight catching daylight on the top rim.
   - **Deep Interior Falloff:** Vertical gradient dropping into dark cavity depth (`#241005` down to `#020000`).
   - **Natural Seating Shelf:** Grounded wooden shelf inside where a hiding animal/character can rest.
