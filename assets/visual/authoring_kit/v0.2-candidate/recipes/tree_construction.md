## 1. Relational Assembly Sequence

1. **Step 1: Structural Trunk & Bough Skeleton:**
   - Draw the trunk with a grounded, weighted root flare.
   - Extend the trunk into 2–4 structural boughs that reach directly into the intended canopy zones.
2. **Step 2: Deep Under-Canopy Silhouette:**
   - Place a rich, deep-toned foliage mass behind the boughs, establishing depth and framing the branch forks.
3. **Step 3: Primary Supported Foliage Masses:**
   - Construct the midtone foliage masses so they rest visibly on and wrap around the supporting boughs.
4. **Step 4: Sunlit Form Volume & Leaf Edges:**
   - Apply diffuse top-left daylighting across the upper surfaces of the foliage masses.
   - Add organic leaf notches and contour breaks along the silhouette.
