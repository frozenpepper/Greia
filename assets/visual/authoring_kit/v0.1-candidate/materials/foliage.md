# Greia Material Guide — Foliage

**Status:** Candidate Authoring Guidance (v0.1)  
**Parent Authority:** `docs/design/GREIA_ART_GRAPHICS_SYSTEM.md`

---

## 1. Visual Philosophy: Matte Storybook, Not Glossy Plastic

Greia foliage embodies a warm, tactile, storybook felt-board aesthetic (inspired by classic European picture books, Tove Jansson, Eric Carle, and Studio Ghibli nature backgrounds).

### Key Rules
- **Matte Form Shading:** Treat foliage as broad, soft planar masses. Use diffuse top-left lighting and soft underside shade.
- **NO Specular Highlight Blobs:** Never place large, pale, high-contrast ellipses on canopy lobes. Specular highlights make foliage look like inflated vinyl or glossy plastic.
- **Organic Edge Variation:** Break mechanical geometric arcs with subtle, deliberate leaf notches and silhouette crests.
- **Asymmetric Shadow Crevices:** Do not draw symmetrical, curved dark strokes in crevices (which can accidentally read as smiling cartoon mouths). Use subtle, angular, botanical shadow breaks.

## 3. Massing & Silhouette Construction

1. **Hierarchy:** Divide the canopy into 3–5 distinct mass groups (Back shadow volume, Core midtone body, Front sunlit billows).
2. **Species Grammar:**
   - **Broad Oak:** Horizontal spreading lobes, low center of gravity, heavy sheltering canopy.
   - **Slender Birch:** Upright, airy, separated leaf clumps with sky showing through branches.
   - **Willow / Weeping:** Cascading vertical teardrop masses with soft hanging tips.
   - **Ground Bush:** Wide, low dome resting flat on the ground plane.
3. **Scale Guardrail:** At 64px, the primary mass silhouette must remain instantly identifiable.
