# Greia Material Guide — Wood & Bark

**Status:** Candidate Authoring Guidance (v0.1)

## 1. Grounding & Structural Logic

Wood in Greia represents warmth, stability, and child-safe organic architecture.

### Key Rules
- **Outward Root Flaring:** The base of every standing tree trunk must flare *outward* into the soil ($x$ width expands at base), resting completely flat along the ground line ($y=455$).
- **NO Inward-Tapering Bases:** Never taper trunks inward at the contact line, as this makes objects appear to float in the air.
- **Contact Occlusion:** The flat root base must directly overlap the tight dark contact shadow ellipse (`#1C120A` opacity 0.35).
- **Structural Boughs:** Trunks must naturally branch out into strong boughs that visibly support canopy masses rather than abruptly disappearing into a ball of leaves.

## 2. Bark Shading & Grain Language

- **Planar Shading:** Use 3 distinct vertical zones:
  - Left lit edge: warm honey/cedar tone (`#A86B42` / `#BD845A`)
  - Central body: warm chestnut midtone (`#7E4826` / `#653416`)
  - Right shadow edge: deep umber (`#45200A` / `#2D1204`)
- **Restrained Grain Ridges:** Add 2–4 subtle, flowing grain lines following the trunk curve. Avoid dense hatching or noisy wood-grain scribbles.
