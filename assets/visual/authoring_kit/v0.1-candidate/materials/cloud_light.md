# Greia Material Guide — Cloud & Light

**Status:** Candidate Authoring Guidance (v0.1)  
**Parent Authority:** `docs/design/GREIA_ART_GRAPHICS_SYSTEM.md`

---

## 1. Soft Volumetric Clouds

- **Spherical Billow Construction:** Compose clouds of 3–5 overlapping rounded domes.
- **Soft Sky Shading:** Use gentle sky-blue underbelly shading (`#B8DEF2` / `#98C8E4`) and warm white sunlit crests (`#FFFFFF` / `#FAFDFE`).
- **Matte Sunlight Sheen:** Avoid harsh specular highlights. Keep light transitions soft and gentle.

---

## 2. Atmosphere & Sunlight Rays

- **Restrained Scene Opacity:** Sunlight shafts must enhance atmosphere without washing out scene elements. Cap cumulative ray opacity at 0.45 in compositions.
- **Feathered Radiant Beams:** Use multi-beam angular shafts with smooth alpha falloffs.
