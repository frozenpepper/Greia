## 1. Form & Geological Facets

Greia stones and boulders represent solid, ancient, friendly river and glacial formations.

### Key Rules
- **Flat Ground Seating:** The bottom of rock clusters must be flat ($y=226$) and anchored into the soil with a dark contact shadow.
- **Soft Planar Facets:** Divide stones into 2–3 clear planes (lit top-left plane, soft face, deep shadow side).
- **Softer Seam Lines:** Avoid harsh black or industrial facet creases. Use soft graphite/slate crease lines (`#303C3E` opacity 0.45) with rounded corners.

---

## 2. Integrated Velvet Moss

- **Integrated Contours:** Moss must hug the top contours and creep naturally into crevices rather than sitting as a detached, floating green cap.
- **Palette:** Warm velvet moss green (`#88DC76` sunlit top, `#4CAF50` midtone, `#2E7D32` shade edge).
