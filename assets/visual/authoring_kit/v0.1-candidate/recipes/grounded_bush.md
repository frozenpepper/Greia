## 1. Construction Rules

1. **Flat Base Seating:** The bottom must sit completely flat on $y=226$ across its full width, directly anchored in the contact shadow.
2. **Plump Volumetric Massing:** Use 3–4 rounded, plush foliage lobes with clear front/back depth planes.
3. **Restrained Accents:** Sprinkle 3–5 simple 3-to-5-petal woodland blossoms or small wild berries nestled cleanly on the foliage planes.
4. **No Smile Marks:** Keep shadow crevices subtle and asymmetric.
