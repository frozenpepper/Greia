## 1. Step-by-Step Tree Assembly

1. **Step 1: Ground Contact Shadow**
   - Place a soft ambient ellipse (`rx=140..150, ry=18..22`, opacity `0.18`) and a tight dark contact ellipse (`rx=105..115, ry=10..12`, opacity `0.35`) at ground line ($y=454..456$).
2. **Step 2: Outward-Flared Trunk & Boughs**
   - Draw trunk with wide outward base flare ($x=160..415$) resting flat on $y=455$.
   - Branch upward into 2–3 structural boughs supporting the canopy zones.
   - Apply warm 3-tone planar bark shading and restrained grain ridges.
3. **Step 3: Back Under-Canopy Shadow Mass**
   - Lay down deep forest shadow silhouette (`#103E15` / `#124416`) providing rich background depth behind boughs.
4. **Step 4: Midtone Core Billows**
   - Add 2–3 large, lush organic foliage domes (`#369240` / `#48AA4E`) shaped specifically for the target species.
5. **Step 5: Front Sunlit Crest Masses**
   - Add sunlit upper-left crown crests (`#6CC65E` / `#88D678`).
   - Use soft diffuse highlights (`#D5F7C8`, opacity 0.55), avoiding glossy specular blobs.
6. **Step 6: Foliage Crevice Depth**
   - Add subtle, asymmetric dark breaks (`#103E15`, opacity 0.40) between major overlapping lobes.

## 2. Species Differentiation Guide

- **Broad Oak:** Horizontal spreading canopy (aspect ratio ~1.3:1), thick heavy trunk, low center of gravity.
- **Slender Birch:** Vertical silhouette (aspect ratio ~0.8:1), slender bowed trunk, airy separated foliage puffs with visible branch gaps.
- **Interactive Hollow Tree:** Asymmetric ancient trunk deformed organically around a deep dark cavity, with thick wooden rim and sheltering canopy overhead.
