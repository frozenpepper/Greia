## 1. True Cavity Depth vs. Flat Oval Cutouts

An interactive hollow in Greia must read as a **deep, inviting, child-accessible shelter** (e.g., hiding spot in *Nascondino nel Bosco*), not a black oval stamped onto a tree.

### Construction Steps
1. **Trunk Deformation:** Bulge the trunk contour outward around the cavity area ($y=300..415$).
2. **Thick Wooden Rim:** Draw a substantial outer bark rim (`#2B1406`) framing the opening.
3. **Beveled Top Rim Highlight:** Add a warm wood highlight (`#C48858`, opacity 0.8) on the top rim where daylight catches the wood edge.
4. **Bottom Rim Shelf:** Add a grounded wooden shelf / floor (`#754320`, opacity 0.7) where a character or hiding animal can sit.
5. **Inner Cavity Gradient:** Use a deep vertical gradient (`#1A0C04` top shadow down to `#050201` pitch black depth).
6. **Interaction Readability at 64px:** The dark cavity framed by the warm rim must remain clearly recognizable as an interactive opening even at low resolution.
