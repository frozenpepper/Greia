## Overview

Workshop v0.2 is the preferred authoring toolbox for Greia workshop and creative studio environments. It provides **24 finished semantic assets** and **2 authoring ingredients** designed with tactile craftsman joinery, warm daylighting, and storybook Gouache material differentiation.

### Key Characteristics
- **Toolbox Architecture:** The pack is an authoring library, not a rigid single room. Activity authors can mix and match furniture, creative tools, and lighting.
- **Meaningful Coexistence:** Rather than forcing a 19-for-19 replacement, 5 distinct pairs coexist where their authoring utility differs:
  - `workshop.shelf.open` (open bracket shelf) vs `workshop.shelf.cubby` (closed wall cubby)
  - `workshop.blocks.house` (assembled structure) vs `workshop.blocks.loose` (loose blocks pile)
  - `workshop.note.cork_tile` (cork-backed tile) vs `workshop.note.pinned` (lightweight sticky memo)
  - `workshop.desk_lamp.articulated` (architect lamp, >=128px) vs `workshop.desk_lamp.simple` (compact lamp, <=64px)
  - `workshop.window_light.wide` (broad sunbeam with motes) vs `workshop.window_light.narrow` (focused layered beam)
- **Authoring Ingredients:** Curated reference/accent pieces: `ingredient_wood_joint.svg` and `ingredient_paint_dabs.svg`.
