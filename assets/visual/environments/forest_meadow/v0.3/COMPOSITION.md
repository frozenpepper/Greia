# Forest / Meadow v0.3 Composition Guidelines

## Hierarchy & Depth Layers

1. **Sky & Far Horizon (Depth Layer 0-1):**
   - `cloud_large.svg`, `cloud_small.svg` (Sky background, soft Gouache daylighting)
   - `distant_hills.svg` (Rolling meadow horizon)
   - `distant_treeline.svg` (Distant pine & deciduous ridge rhythm)
2. **Atmospheric Lighting & Fog:**
   - `sunlight_rays.svg` (Warm golden morning godrays filtering from top-left)
   - `fog_strip.svg` (Soft morning ground mist layer)
3. **Midground Botanical & Terrain (Depth Layer 2):**
   - `tree_hero.svg` (Mature oak hero landmark)
   - `tree_secondary.svg` (Slender woodland birch)
   - `tree_sapling.svg` (Bright young sapling)
   - `tree_willow.svg` (Cascading weeping willow)
   - `tree_interactive_hollow.svg` (Playfield interactive tree with living cavity)
4. **Foreground & Playfield (Depth Layer 3-4):**
   - `grass_edge.svg` (Meadow turf edge)
   - `ground_dirt_patch.svg` (Footpath clearing patch)
   - `bush_round.svg`, `bush_flowering.svg`, `bush_low.svg`
   - `fern_cluster.svg`, `flower_patch.svg`, `mushroom_cluster.svg`
   - `rock_cluster_large.svg`, `rock_cluster_small.svg`
   - `fallen_log.svg`, `stump.svg`
   - `shadow_contact_small.svg`, `shadow_contact_wide.svg`
5. **Ambient FX:**
   - `fx_fireflies.svg`, `fx_dust_motes.svg`, `fx_falling_leaves.svg`
