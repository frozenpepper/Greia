# Generic Core Essentials — Composition Guidelines

Batch A assets are designed for broad cross-Activity composition:

1. **Room Architecture & Entryways:**
   - Place `door_timber_closed.svg` or `door_timber_ajar.svg` against `wall_base.svg` or `room_wall_base.svg`.
   - Use `opening_arch_simple.svg` as a room-to-room spatial divider.
2. **Reading & Study Corners:**
   - Pair `armchair_soft_reading.svg` or `chair_spindle_wood.svg` with `bookcase_tall_open.svg` and `plant_potted_fern.svg`.
   - Place `book_open_picture_book.svg` and `books_stack_mixed.svg` on tables, daybeds, or rugs.
3. **Tabletop & Dining Layouts:**
   - Use `plate_ceramic_empty.svg`, `bowl_ceramic_empty.svg`, and `cup_saucer_ceramic.svg` as neutral bases for Activity-local food, fruit, or item placement.
4. **Maker & Manipulative Activities:**
   - Use `gear_wooden.svg`, `gears_interlocking_pair.svg`, and `dial_hands_blank.svg` as interactive machine puzzle components.
