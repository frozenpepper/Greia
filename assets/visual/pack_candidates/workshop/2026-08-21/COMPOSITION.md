# Workshop Composition Guidelines

## Layout Hierarchy

1. **Background Layer (y: 0 - 420):**
   - `wall_base.svg` forms the warm plaster upper wall and wainscot paneling.
   - `window.svg` placed at top-left or top-center to establish the exterior light source.
   - `pegboard.svg`, `shelf.svg`, and `pinboard_note.svg` provide mid-wall organization without visual clutter.
2. **Atmosphere / Lighting Layer:**
   - `window_light.svg` casts diagonally from the window aperture across the wall and floor.
3. **Floor / Playfield Layer (y: 400 - 600):**
   - `floor_base.svg` provides the wide-plank oak foundation.
   - `rug.svg` centers the activity play area.
   - `workbench.svg` anchors the main workspace with grounded splayed legs.
   - `stool.svg` and `easel.svg` frame the workbench on the left and right.
4. **Tabletop & Prop Layer:**
   - `desk_lamp.svg`, `pencil_cup.svg`, `paint_jars.svg`, `paint_palette.svg`, and `paper_stack.svg` sit cleanly on the workbench top slab.
   - `blocks.svg` and `storage_box.svg` ground the floor playfield.
