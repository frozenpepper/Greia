# Greia trademarks — quick guide

**Status:** project policy clarified 2026-10-27. Greia remains pre-release;
trademark ownership, clearance, possible registration and legal review are
pending. No registered trademark or formal certification programme is claimed.

Greia's code is open-source (see [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md) and [LICENSE](LICENSE)).
**Anyone can install, modify and sell the covered software**, including
computers with Greia preinstalled, subject to the applicable licenses.
No commercial license, fee or royalty is due to Greia for exercising those
software rights.

## Free and permitted

- Truthfully state **“Runs Greia OS”**, **“Powered by Greia OS”** or
  **“Compatible with Greia”**, even on a product box or commercial listing,
  without implying that the product is officially endorsed by Greia.
- Show Greia in accurate tutorials, screenshots, reviews and school/NGO
  materials; credit the project.
- Redistribute authentic unmodified Greia with its in-software branding
  and required notices intact.
- Develop Activities and forks under their applicable licenses. Give a
  substantial derivative its own product identity, while saying truthfully
  that it is *based on Greia*.

## Ask for permission for official branding

Do not present your own product as an **official Greia device**, make the
Greia logo the prominent manufacturer/box brand, or use **“Official Greia
Partner”**, **“Greia Certified”**, **“Greia Approved”** or an equivalent
official endorsement/badge without a written agreement.

Such optional agreements may cover logo use, co-branding, integration,
custom Activities, testing, support or negotiated commercial fees. They
never change the underlying open-source rights.

We deliberately distinguish a **truthful mention of the software on a box**
(which is free) from an **officially branded or certified product**
(which may need a commercial agreement).

The [full Greia Licensing and Trademark Policy](docs/governance/LICENSING_AND_TRADEMARK.md)
is authoritative; [ADR 0024](docs/adr/0024-project-licensing-and-trademark-boundary.md)
explains the architectural decision. Contact the project maintainers through
existing repository channels about a proposed official partnership.
